About

I am a professional film and television editor based in Toronto, Canada. As a freelance editor I work in several genres, including television,documentaries, short and feature films.

My work has been screened at film festivals internationally and is broadcast on Sundance Now, CBC, HBO, Discovery Channel, Cooking Channel, History Channel, Netflix, SyFy, and Vice.

Education

  • 2006: Graduate Certificate: Advanced Television and Film, Sheridan College, Oakville, Ontario
  • 2004: Bachelor of Fine Arts: Media Arts and Digital Technology, Alberta University of the Arts, Calgary, Alberta
